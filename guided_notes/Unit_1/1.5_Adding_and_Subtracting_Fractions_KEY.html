1.5  Adding & Subtracting Fractions

Guided Notes - Unit 1: Foundations - DHS Algebra 1
Teacher Key - answers in red
Objectives
  • Add and subtract fractions with like denominators
  • Add and subtract fractions with unlike denominators
  • Add and subtract mixed numbers
  • Subtract a larger fraction from a smaller one and get a negative answer

Warm-Up

1. Monique ate 1 slice of pepperoni and 2 slices of cheese. Both pizzas were cut into 8 equal slices. How much pizza did she eat?

Answer: 38    How did you know? the slices are the same size, so just add the numerators

2. Jack ate 1 slice of a pepperoni pizza cut into 3 pieces and 1 slice of a cheese pizza cut into 4 pieces. How much pizza did he eat?

Answer: 712    Why is this one harder? the pieces are different sizes, so they must be rewritten first

1.5.1Like Denominators

Same denominator means the pieces are the same size.
  • Keep the denominator.
  • Add or subtract the numerators only.
  • Last step of every problem: simplify.
Gotcha: numerators only
Do not add the denominators. 27 + 37 is not 514.
EX 1. 27 + 37      EX 2. 1112 − 512      EX 3. 59 + 79

27 + 37 = 57

1112 − 512 = 612 = 12

59 + 79 = 129 = 43 = 113

You Try: 1015 − 415 = 615 = 25

1.5.2Unlike Denominators

Different denominators mean the pieces are different sizes, so we rewrite them first. A common denominator is a number both denominators divide into evenly.
The smallest one: list the multiples of each and take the first they share.

Multiples of 3: 3, 6, 9, 12, 15…

Multiples of 4: 4, 8, 12, 16…  →  smallest: 12

A faster way to get one, and the way we will use. Multiply the two denominators.
  1. Multiply the denominators. That product is your common denominator.
  2. Multiply the top and bottom of the first fraction by the second denominator.
  3. Multiply the top and bottom of the second fraction by the first denominator.
  4. Add or subtract the numerators. Keep the denominator.
  5. Simplify.
Why does b × d always work? it is a multiple of b and a multiple of d, so both divide into it evenly
Is it always the smallest? no - only when the denominators share no factor
Gotcha: top and bottom, same number
44 = 1, so multiplying by it does not change the value. Multiply only the bottom and you have changed the fraction into a different number.
EX 4. 13 + 14

Multiply the denominators: 3 × 4 = 12

Rewrite: 13 × 44 = ___12     14 × 33 = ___12

Add: 412 + 312 = 712

EX 5. 34 − 25

Multiply the denominators: 4 × 5 = 20

Answer: 1520 − 820 = 720

EX 6. 78 − 14   -   the two ways give different denominators.

Multiply the denominators: 8 × 4 = 32

2832 − 832 = ___32     GCF of 20 and 32: 4     Answer: 58

Listing multiples would have given 8, because 8 is already a multiple of 4: 78 − ___8 = 58

Same answer either way? yes

The trade
Multiplying the denominators means no thinking at the start and a little simplifying at the end. An unsimplified answer is a wrong answer.
Think. Example 4 used 3 and 4, and multiplying gave the smallest denominator. Example 6 used 8 and 4, and it did not. What is different?

3 and 4 share no factor; 8 and 4 share a factor of 4

EX 7. 56 + 79   -   neither denominator divides into the other.

Multiply the denominators: 6 × 9 = 54

___54 + ___54 = ___54

Simplify, then write as a mixed number: 8754 = 2918 = 11118

4554 + 4254 = 8754

When the Answer Is Negative

If the fraction you start with is smaller than the one you take away, the answer is negative. Finding the denominator does not change. The only new part is subtracting the numerators, which you did in 1.1.
−512 and −512 are the same number. Write the sign in front.
−279 means the whole number and the fraction are both negative
EX 9. 38 − 78   -   the denominators already match.

Subtract the numerators: 3 − 7 = −4

Answer: −48 = −12

EX 10. 14 − 23

Multiply the denominators: 4 × 3 = 12     ___12 − ___12

Subtract the numerators: 3 − 8 = −5

Answer: −512

You Try. 25 − 34

5 × 4 = 20    820 − 1520 = −720

1.5.3Adding & Subtracting Mixed Numbers

Why not just add the whole numbers and then the fractions? It breaks on 614 − 338, because 14 is smaller than 38 and you have to borrow. Turn them into fractions first instead.
  1. Convert each mixed number to an improper fraction: abc = a × c + bc
  2. Multiply the denominators, then add or subtract.
  3. Simplify. Convert back to a mixed number if you need to.
A whole number is a fraction over 1, so 8 = 81.
EX 11. 213 + 156

Convert: 213 = ___3     156 = ___6

Denominators: 3 × 6 = 18     ___18 + ___18 = ___18

Simplify, then back to a mixed number: 7518 = 256 = 416

213 = 73 = 4218    156 = 116 = 3318
